# Ice Cream Rush Visual System

- Palette: ink `#35283f`, cream `#fff8ee`, pink `#ff6489`, yellow `#ffd45f`, mint `#64d8b1`, blue `#65bfe6`, purple `#735ad7`.
- Shape language: rounded 14–36 px corners, dark 2–4 px outlines, flat fills, short downward shadows.
- Characters: oversized rounded heads, dot eyes, one-line smiles, solid-color shirts, six controlled skin/hair combinations.
- Icons: 52 px source canvas in 64–86 px controls; every control retains a minimum 48 px hit target.
- Motion: 100–220 ms UI actions, soft idle floats, stronger combo pulse only at x3/x4. Reduced Motion removes decorative animation.
- Typography: Fredoka 600/700, self-hosted Latin subset, English uppercase labels with generous tracking.
- Backgrounds: mint parlor walls and warm counters; gameplay information always sits on a quiet high-contrast surface.
