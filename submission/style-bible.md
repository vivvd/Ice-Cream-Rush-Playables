# Ice Cream Rush Visual System

- Palette: ink `#35283f`, cream `#fff8ee`, pink `#ff6489`, yellow `#ffd45f`, mint `#64d8b1`, blue `#65bfe6`, purple `#735ad7`.
- Art direction: original polished 2D game illustration, rounded dark-plum outline, soft cel/gouache shading, candy color accents, clean silhouettes, no embedded text or logos.
- Product sprites: 19 transparent WebP layers on normalized 256×256 canvases. Bases, scoops, toppings, drinks, and Cinnamon Rolls share a centered optical anchor and remain readable in cards and tickets.
- Equipment: four transparent 256×256 WebP icons, simplified for recognition in the permanent 48 px dock.
- Characters: eight identities at 384×384, each with identity-preserving happy, worried, angry, and urgent variants. CSS provides the continuous red stress aura and Reduced Motion-safe urgent state.
- Scenes: matched 1536×1024 landscape and 1024×1536 portrait parlor interiors. The mint center remains visually quiet behind gameplay.
- Code UI: rounded 14–36 px corners, 2–4 px plum outlines, short downward shadows, minimum 48×48 px input targets.
- Campaign UI: purple Level cards, muted locked days, mint completed days, a persistent cash-goal meter, and a distinct gold Endless state after Day 25.
- Economy labels: dark high-contrast price badges use tabular figures at a minimum visual size of roughly 11 px; five-digit equipment prices remain visible in the right dock.
- Motion: 100–280 ms UI actions, an item-served shrink/pop, soft idle movement, and stronger combo emphasis at x3/x4. Reduced Motion removes decorative pulsing.
- Typography: self-hosted Fredoka 600/700 Latin subset, English uppercase labels with generous tracking.
